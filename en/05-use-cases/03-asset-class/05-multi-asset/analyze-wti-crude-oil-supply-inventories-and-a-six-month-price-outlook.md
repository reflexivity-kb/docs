## Prompt used

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Analyze the WTI crude oil supply-demand balance and the price outlook for the next six months.

## The research workflow

A commodity outlook should connect current prices, the supply-demand gap, inventory movements and possible disruptions rather than extrapolate the last price move. Alfred can structure a base case, upside risk and downside risk.

## Source-period findings

In the October 8 snapshot, the WTI futures close was $91.49 per barrel. The example compared 2026 supply of 101.1 million barrels/day with demand of 102.4 million, and a 2027 supply-surplus scenario; its central narrative was gradual easing from high prices as disrupted supply recovered.

## Interpretation and limitations

These are October 2026 source assumptions, not a current forecast. Supply interruptions, the Strait of Hormuz, shipping costs and demand weakness could substantially change the outcome; Brent and WTI are distinct benchmarks.
