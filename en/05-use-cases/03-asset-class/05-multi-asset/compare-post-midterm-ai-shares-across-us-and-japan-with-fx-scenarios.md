## Prompt used

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Following the Democratic midterm win, compare US and Japanese AI and semiconductor stocks and present outlook scenarios for the sector and USD/JPY.

## Source-period research

The November 5 analysis contrasts a decline in Japanese AI-related shares with gains in the US broad indices, while NVIDIA itself fell. It separates data-center regulatory exposure from wider macro relief and describes base, positive and negative scenarios for stocks and USD/JPY.

## How to interpret it

The original used preliminary election-seat counts that changed later; do not treat them as final. Scenario probabilities are the researcher's subjective assumptions rather than probabilities estimated from market prices.
