## Research question and workflow

1. Compare one-week implied volatility and realized S&P 500 volatility across the five prior US midterms; do not treat VIX and realized volatility as equivalent.
2. Inspect supporting company, theme, event and market inputs rather than using the summary alone.

## What the example shows

Compare one-week implied volatility and realized S&P 500 volatility across the five prior US midterms; do not treat VIX and realized volatility as equivalent.

Across five prior midterms, pre-election mean VIX was 18.53 versus 17.02 afterward, while realized S&P volatility sometimes rose after the vote; only five events were sampled.
