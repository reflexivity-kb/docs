## Prompt used

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Summarize how US bond, FX and stock markets responded to the above-consensus October CPI released on November 10.

## Research workflow

Use the CPI surprise as a common starting point, then measure Treasury-yield moves in basis points, dollar moves in percent or exchange-rate terms, and equity index returns over a consistent session.

## Source-period results

In the November 11 report, headline CPI was 0.4% month-on-month versus 0.3% consensus. The source's same-day market report put the US 2-year yield up 20.1 bp, 10-year up 13.8 bp, USD/JPY at 158.74 and the S&P 500 down about 0.4%.

## Limits and interpretation

The source noted that internal time series stopped on November 9, so November 10 market figures were taken from news. ADP employment and Fed speakers were concurrent events; all price moves cannot be attributed to CPI alone.
