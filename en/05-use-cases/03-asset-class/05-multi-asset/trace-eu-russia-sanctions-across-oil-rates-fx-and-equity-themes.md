## Prompt used

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Analyze how wider EU sanctions targeting Russia's defense industry could affect equities, bonds, foreign exchange and crude oil, including affected stock themes and companies.

## The research workflow

Sanctions aimed at a military procurement network do not necessarily restrict all listed companies or energy shipments directly. The useful research task is to separate direct effects from indirect channels such as supply risk, fiscal spending, inflation expectations and defense procurement.

## Source-period findings

The October 13 source separated crude oil's geopolitical premium from sanctions targeting defense procurement, then compared Bunds and Treasuries, EUR/USD, defense-company themes and oil-services names. Aerospace and defense names were not assumed to rise merely because military spending could increase.

## Interpretation and limitations

Market moves also depend on other conflicts, peace negotiations and refinery disruptions. The example is a dated scenario analysis, not proof that one sanctions decision caused all observed returns.
