## Prompt used

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Summarize the current Japanese market environment.

## Source-period research

The November 4 briefing combines the previous trading day's Nikkei and TOPIX closes with overnight futures, US rates, yen moves and the election backdrop. The Tokyo market was shut on the November 3 holiday; Nikkei's November 2 close was 63,081.86.

## How to interpret it

A morning preview can mix spot closes, overnight futures and forecasts. Always label the observation date and source of each value, and verify live quotes separately before trading.
