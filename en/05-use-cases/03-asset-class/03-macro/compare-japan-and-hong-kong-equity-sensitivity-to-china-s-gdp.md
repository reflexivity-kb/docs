## Prompt used

> [!IMPORTANT]
> How much could today's China GDP release affect Japanese equities, and how does Hong Kong's stock market interpret China's growth?

## The research workflow

A Chinese growth release does not affect all neighboring markets equally. Evaluate the channels through exporters, materials and tourism-sensitive Japanese names, while comparing Hong Kong's closer exposure to mainland growth and policy expectations.

## Source-period findings

Ahead of the October 19 Q3 2026 release, consensus was 4.4% year-on-year versus 4.8% previously. In 11 selected past growth-slowdown episodes, the source reported the Nikkei 225 median +1.08% one week later and +3.31% three months later, versus Hang Seng median -0.20% after one week and -0.87% after one month.

## Interpretation and limitations

The example was produced before the release; consensus is not the actual GDP print. Eleven selected episodes are a small conditional sample and do not establish causation or predict the next release's market return.
