## Research question and workflow

1. Compare historical reactions and economic exposure rather than assuming Chinese growth data affects both equity markets equally.
2. Inspect supporting company, theme, event and market inputs rather than using the summary alone.

## What the example shows

Compare historical reactions and economic exposure rather than assuming Chinese growth data affects both equity markets equally.

The 19 October preview cited China Q3 GDP consensus growth of 4.4%, down from 4.8% in the prior quarter, and judged Hong Kong shares more sensitive than Japanese shares.
