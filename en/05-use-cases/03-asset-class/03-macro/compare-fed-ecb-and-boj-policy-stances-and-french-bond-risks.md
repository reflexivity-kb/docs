## Research workflow

The October 28 report compared tightening bias and likely meeting decisions for three central banks, then extended the question to French government-bond credit concerns and spread sensitivity.

## Dated example

The original used the Fed target range, ECB deposit facility rate and BOJ overnight call-rate target rather than treating the three policy-rate series as identical. Energy-related inflation risk was a key common scenario.

## How to interpret it

This is a meeting-eve historical scenario, not today's policy-rate table. Cross-country headline rate comparisons require correct instrument definitions, dates and pricing of forward rates.
