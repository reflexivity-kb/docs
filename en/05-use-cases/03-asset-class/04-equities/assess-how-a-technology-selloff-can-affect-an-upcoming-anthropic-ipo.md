## Prompt used

> [!IMPORTANT]
> How could the sharp decline in US data-center related stocks affect the IPO price of Anthropic?

## Source-period research

The November 6 analysis distinguishes offering valuation pressure from the risk of delaying the listing. Anthropic's amended filing had not specified the offering range at that point; the original also cited a drop in a prediction-market probability for a November listing.

## How to interpret it

This is a November 6 historical snapshot; subsequent amendments may have changed the offering's terms. Prediction-market prices are sentiment indicators, not validated forecasts or regulatory decisions.
