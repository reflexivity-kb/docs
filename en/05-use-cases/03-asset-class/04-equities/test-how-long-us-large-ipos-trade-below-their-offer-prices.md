## Prompt used

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Analyze whether any major US IPOs over the past 20 years traded below the offer price for their entire public history.

## Source-period research

The November 9 analysis sampled 36 major US IPOs. It found two names, StubHub and Venture Global, whose closing prices never exceeded their initial offer prices under the sample definition. Twenty-one of 36 were below their offer prices at the last observed date; the median return was -16.4%.

## How to interpret it

Distinguish 'never above offer at the close' from 'currently below offer'; they are different measures. The 36 selected IPOs are not all US IPOs, and older IPO prices or corporate actions may require adjusted historical series.
