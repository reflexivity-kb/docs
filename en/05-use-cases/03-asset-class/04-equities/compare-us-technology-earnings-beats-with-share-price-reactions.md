## Prompt used

> [!IMPORTANT]
> 先週と今週に発表された主要な米国IT企業の決算発表の結果をまとめてください。売上高、利益に加え、予想EPSと実際の差を表示してください。またそれぞれについてその後の市場の反応と、各社の今後の戦略と業績見込みについても記載してください。

## Research workflow

Earnings beats do not necessarily produce positive stock returns. The original compared reported revenue, profits, actual versus consensus EPS, market reactions and forward strategies across 12 US technology companies.

## Dated example

Of the 12 companies reporting during October 19–28, 11 beat expected EPS, yet reactions diverged. The source highlighted Azure +48% and Google Cloud +103% growth, alongside investor concern that accelerating capex could weaken free cash flow.

## How to interpret it

Price windows and the accounting basis of consensus and reported EPS must match. After-hours and next-day price responses should not be mixed without a timestamp, and longer-term guidance is separate from a one-quarter beat.
