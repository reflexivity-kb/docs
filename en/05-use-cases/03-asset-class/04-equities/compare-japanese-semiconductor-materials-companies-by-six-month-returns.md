## Prompt used

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Among Japanese firms in the Semiconductor Materials theme, analyze the previous six months of performance and explain the strongest and weakest companies.

## Research workflow

Use Knowledge Graph's Semiconductor Materials theme to identify Japanese members, select a consistent 6-month return window and inspect the drivers behind winning and losing constituents.

## Source-period findings

Of 75 theme names, 32 were Japanese. The source selected 15 with the strongest thematic exposure for April 23–October 22, 2026. Ibiden rose 54.5% and Sumitomo Bakelite 52.5%, while C. Uyemura fell 22.8%; the theme basket gained about 3.2%.

## Interpretation and limitations

The 15-stock sample is selected by graph relevance, not market cap. Earnings Recap for those Japanese names was unavailable in the cited analysis, so causal explanations rely on news and cannot establish why prices moved.
