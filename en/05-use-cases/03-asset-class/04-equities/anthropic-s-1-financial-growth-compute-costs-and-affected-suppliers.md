## Prompt used

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Analyze Anthropic's S-1 filing, its recent and projected financials, and companies likely to benefit from or be affected by its IPO.

## Research workflow

This case reads Anthropic's October 26 SEC S-1 to compare revenue growth with large net losses and future compute infrastructure obligations, and maps potential implications for chip and cloud suppliers.

## Dated example

The source-period analysis reported 2025 revenue around $4.6B and 2026 first-half revenue around $16.3B, alongside significant noncash loss adjustments. Broadcom, Alphabet, Microsoft, Amazon and AMD were assessed as potential compute beneficiaries.

## How to interpret it

Contract totals in the email combine firm minimums and potential future purchases, so they must not be added indiscriminately. S-1 was preliminary: the October 26 statement did not specify an offering price range. Verify operational losses separately from fair-value accounting adjustments.

Official filing: [SEC Anthropic Form S-1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026067168/anthropicpbc-sx1.htm)
