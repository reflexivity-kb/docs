## Prompt used

> [!IMPORTANT]
> After NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang resigned, compare large founder-CEO departures over the past 20 years, one-, three- and six-month stock performance, and potential AI/data-center spillovers.

## Research workflow

Compare planned founder succession with sudden departures before averaging outcomes. The November 13 study covers Apple (2011), Oracle (2014), Wynn (2018), Alphabet (2019) and Amazon (2021), with Intel as a non-founder reference. NVIDIA's CEO departure was publicly announced November 12.

## Source-period results

The five founder cases had median price returns of +5.2% after one month, +0.1% after three months and +6.9% after six months, as calculated in the source. The same report compared NVIDIA with AMD, Broadcom and data-center infrastructure stocks to separate governance risk from industry demand.

## Limits and interpretation

Five cases are not statistically representative, and planned succession differs from abrupt exit. The stock windows use trading days and exclude dividends. Official NVIDIA release and SEC 8-K independently substantiate the management change, not the source's causal stock-market interpretation.

Official confirmation: [NVIDIA CEO transition](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition/)
