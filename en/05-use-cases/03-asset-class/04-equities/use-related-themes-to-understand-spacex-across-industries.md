## Research workflow

An industry label alone can hide a firm's multiple activities. Open SpaceX in Asset Overview, select Connections and Related Themes, and inspect the earnings summary to connect its space, connectivity and AI businesses.

## Source-period findings

The October 22 QUICK screen showed the top three related themes connected to space and AI-related themes at ranks four, six and seven, followed by an earnings-summary screen. It is a navigation and research example, not a recommendation.

## Interpretation and limitations

Rank denotes thematic relationship, not revenue proportion, portfolio weight or stock return. Consult the dated company filing for operating-segment evidence; changes in interface and available coverage are possible.
