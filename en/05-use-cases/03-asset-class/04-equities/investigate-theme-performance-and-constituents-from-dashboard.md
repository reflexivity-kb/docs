## The research workflow

A trending market theme is a research starting point, not a conclusion. Open Dashboard's Theme Performance, inspect the theme's related companies and compare the short-term move with a one-year window.

## Source-period findings

The October 7 QUICK example used US nuclear-related names that had attracted attention in the prior New York session. The screenshot sequence moved from a theme ranking to constituent details and then to a one-year comparison that was not uniformly positive.

## Interpretation and limitations

The source is a dated interface walkthrough, not a live ranking. Thematic association does not itself prove revenue exposure, profitability or investability.
