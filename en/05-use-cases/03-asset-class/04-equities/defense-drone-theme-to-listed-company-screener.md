## Workflow

A company in the news may be private, as in the 2026-11-16 Saronic Technologies example. Instead of treating the startup as a tradable security, start with the wider Defense Drones investment theme.

## What this example demonstrates

Enter the Japanese search word `防衛` in Search; select the `防衛用ドローン` theme, open the Companies view, then inspect theme Performance and an individual company's Asset Overview. Send the candidate to Watchlist or narrow it with Screener using the asset handoff.

## Important limitations

Being related to a theme does not establish that a company is listed, liquid, or has material revenue exposure. Verify each candidate's listing, business exposure and financial condition.
