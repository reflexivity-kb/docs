## Prompt used

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Prepare an analyst-style report on Salesforce: company overview, recent news, earnings released in the last month, one year of quarterly financials, related themes and their performance, and a one-year daily stock chart.

## Research workflow

One reusable Alfred prompt can request a company profile, dated headlines, earnings recap, standardized quarterly financials, thematic relationships and the stock's 12-month history in a consistent order. Replace the company name to adapt the report.

## Source-period results

The November 10 source covered Salesforce, reporting Q2 FY2027 revenue of $11.35B and year-on-year growth of 10.8%, alongside cRPO, GAAP EPS, free cash flow, AI/SaaS theme returns and a daily stock-price chart.

## Limits and interpretation

GAAP and adjusted EPS are not interchangeable; fiscal quarters may differ from calendar quarters. The prompt is a report template, not evidence that every requested metric is available for every company.
