## Prompt used

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Group the major Japanese AI picks-and-shovels stocks into relative winners and losers after the US midterms, and explain their potential drivers.

## Research workflow

Compare returns from November 2 to 11, not just the sector label. Distinguish firms exposed to new data-center construction from those with more diversified industrial gas, power grid or automation demand.

## Source-period results

Against Nikkei 225 -2.91%, the source showed Nippon Sanso +8.97% and Mitsubishi Electric +3.39%, while Fujikura was -19.97%, Furukawa Electric -13.87%, Ibiden -13.98% and Advantest -12.11%. The source attributed part of the dispersion to capex sensitivity, earnings and capital returns.

## Limits and interpretation

The seven-session period is short; the election, company-specific earnings, commodity prices and FX cannot be cleanly separated. Some negative-return names still outperformed the benchmark, so 'positive' means relative strength in part of the analysis.
