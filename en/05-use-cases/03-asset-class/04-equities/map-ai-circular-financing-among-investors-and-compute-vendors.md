## Prompt used

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Explain in a table the equity investments, financing and business relationships among NVIDIA, Anthropic, Meta, Google, Amazon, Microsoft and related AI firms, using Anthropic's S-1.

## Research workflow

The investigation maps when an AI vendor's shareholders are also paid cloud, GPU or custom-chip suppliers. It follows funding into compute contracts, using Anthropic's 2026 S-1 as the dated example.

## Dated example

The original distinguished about $417B in committed compute payments from wider proposed or optional contract values. Investors/suppliers included Amazon, Google, Microsoft and others, creating questions about concentration, capacity and economic returns.

## How to interpret it

An equity investment, a binding purchase commitment, an option to buy compute and the resulting supplier revenue are not the same cash flow. Do not sum headline amounts, infer double-counted revenue, or assume every contractual dollar is realized.

Official filing: [SEC Anthropic Form S-1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026067168/anthropicpbc-sx1.htm)
