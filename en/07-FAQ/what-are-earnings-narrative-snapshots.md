# What Are Earnings Narrative Snapshots?

Earnings Narrative Snapshots are intended to present earnings as a developing narrative rather than only as a collection of figures. The roadmap describes reading earnings across followed companies with an emphasis on what may come next.

The **July 2026 roadmap** placed the capability on the EOY roadmap. The **August 2026 product update** listed it as “Readying for release.” These are dated status points, not a statement that the feature is available today.

See [Product Roadmap - July 2026](https://github.com/reflexivity-kb/platform/blob/main/en/04-releases/2026-07-product-roadmap.md) and [Product Update Snapshot - August 2026](https://github.com/reflexivity-kb/platform/blob/main/en/04-releases/2026-08-product-update-snapshot.md).
