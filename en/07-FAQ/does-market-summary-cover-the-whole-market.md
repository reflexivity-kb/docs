# Does Market Summary provide a complete picture of the market?

<!-- locale-switcher:start -->

No. Market Summary is a selective overview of noteworthy market and company developments, not a complete inventory of every event, index move, or sector performance measure.

Some editions may place greater emphasis on individual company disclosures than on broad market indicators. For a more comprehensive review or investment decision, cross-check major indices, sector performance, underlying disclosures, publication times, and authoritative current market data.
