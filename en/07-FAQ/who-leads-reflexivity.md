Reflexivity is led by a team with backgrounds in institutional investing, fintech, product, and AI/ML.

## Jan Szilagyi: Chief Executive Officer and Co-Founder

Former Co-CIO at Lombard Odier and portfolio manager at Fortress. The company describes him as holding the world record for the fastest Harvard Economics PhD.

## Giuseppe Sette: President and Co-Founder

Former Co-CIO Global Macro at Lombard Odier, with previous experience at Brevan Howard and Davidson Kempner.

## Armenak Mayalian: Chief Technology Officer and Co-Founder

A five-time fintech founder and former CTO of Sharepay, with more than 20 years of experience scaling financial platforms.

## RJ Assaly: Chief Product Officer

Former Product Lead at Kensho, which was acquired by S&P Global, and previously at AllianceBernstein.

## Katherine Plumlee: Chief Strategy Officer

AI/ML venture investor, builder, and operator. JD, MBA.

Reflexivity's current company website lists the same five leaders and titles.
