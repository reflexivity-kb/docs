[← FAQ](README.md) · [Product Roadmap - July 2026](https://github.com/reflexivity-kb/platform/blob/main/en/04-releases/2026-07-product-roadmap.md)

Prompt Library is intended to let users save frequently used analyses as reusable templates. A saved workflow can then be applied to another company by changing the target rather than rebuilding the analysis from the beginning.

The **July 2026 roadmap** placed Prompt Library on the EOY roadmap. The **August 2026 product update** listed it as “Readying for release.” These are historical planning states rather than a current availability guarantee.

See [Product Roadmap - July 2026](https://github.com/reflexivity-kb/platform/blob/main/en/04-releases/2026-07-product-roadmap.md) and [Product Update Snapshot - August 2026](https://github.com/reflexivity-kb/platform/blob/main/en/04-releases/2026-08-product-update-snapshot.md).
