[← FAQ](README.md) · [Product Roadmap - July 2026](https://github.com/reflexivity-kb/platform/blob/main/en/04-releases/2026-07-product-roadmap.md)

You can ask about access, but the July 2026 roadmap and August 2026 product-update snapshot do **not** define a formal early-access program, eligibility criteria, or guaranteed preview access.

For a specific capability, the safest route is to ask whether it is currently available or entitled for your account. Roadmap timing alone should not be treated as an access commitment.

See [Product Roadmap - July 2026](https://github.com/reflexivity-kb/platform/blob/main/en/04-releases/2026-07-product-roadmap.md) and [Product Update Snapshot - August 2026](https://github.com/reflexivity-kb/platform/blob/main/en/04-releases/2026-08-product-update-snapshot.md).

For current availability or entitlement, contact **jim@reflexivity.com**.
