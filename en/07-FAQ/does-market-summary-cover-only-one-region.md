# Does Market Summary cover news from only one country or region?

<!-- locale-switcher:start -->

[← FAQ](README.md)

Market Summary may include news from multiple countries and regions, and the geographic mix can vary from one summary to another.

A higher proportion of stories about one country does not, by itself, establish that a country-specific filter is configured. When interpreting a summary, check which companies, markets, and observation periods its individual stories refer to. The underlying filter configuration cannot be determined from the summary text alone.
