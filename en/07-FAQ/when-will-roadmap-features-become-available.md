Roadmap timing is a planning target, not a guaranteed release date.

The July 2026 roadmap used two relative planning windows:

- **Coming in the next two months** for @ Tagging in Alfred and Export to PowerPoint & Word.
- **On the roadmap for EOY** for Pulses, Knowledge Graph Explorer, Earnings Narrative Snapshots, and Prompt Library.

Those labels describe the roadmap **as of July 2026**. Product priorities, implementation timing, release readiness, and account availability can change after the roadmap is published.

Roadmap material is refreshed as newer approved product information becomes available, while dated roadmap pages preserve the point-in-time view for reference. Later release information may therefore supersede an older roadmap status.

For the full July 2026 view, read [Product Roadmap - July 2026](https://github.com/reflexivity-kb/platform/blob/main/en/04-releases/2026-07-product-roadmap.md).
