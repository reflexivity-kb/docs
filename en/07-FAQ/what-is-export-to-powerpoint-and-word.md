Export to PowerPoint & Word is a planned workflow for turning completed Reflexivity analysis into PowerPoint or Word materials that can be adapted for client-ready documents and corporate branding.

The **July 2026 roadmap** placed it in “Coming in the next two months.” The **August 2026 product update** described it as “Readying for release.” These are dated planning states, not a guarantee of current availability.

See [Product Roadmap - July 2026](https://github.com/reflexivity-kb/platform/blob/main/en/04-releases/2026-07-product-roadmap.md) and [Product Update Snapshot - August 2026](https://github.com/reflexivity-kb/platform/blob/main/en/04-releases/2026-08-product-update-snapshot.md).
