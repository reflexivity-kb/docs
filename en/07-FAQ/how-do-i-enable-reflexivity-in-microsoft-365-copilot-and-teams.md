# How Do I Enable Reflexivity in Microsoft 365 Copilot and Teams?

For Microsoft 365 Copilot, current Reflexivity guidance requires an active Reflexivity subscription and an eligible Microsoft 365 Copilot environment. Existing customers can enable the integration from their Reflexivity account settings and authorize it in Copilot. In Teams, the documented setup flow is to search for Reflexivity in Teams Apps, sign in with the Reflexivity account, and pin Alfred to the relevant chat or channel. Some organizations require Microsoft 365 administrator approval.
