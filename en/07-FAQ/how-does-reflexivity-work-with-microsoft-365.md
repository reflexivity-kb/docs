Reflexivity adds investment intelligence to Microsoft 365 workflows through three connected paths: connect selected SharePoint/OneDrive, Outlook, and OneNote sources to Alfred; use Alfred in Microsoft Teams; and use Reflexivity's Knowledge Graph inside Microsoft 365 Copilot. The goal is to combine internal context with Reflexivity market intelligence without manually moving among files, emails, notes, and research tools.
