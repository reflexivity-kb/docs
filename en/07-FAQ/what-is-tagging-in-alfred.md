@ Tagging is a planned Alfred capability for setting research context directly inside a conversation. The July 2026 roadmap describes tagging a company, theme, or watchlist so Alfred can begin from the intended context instead of requiring the user to restate it.

In the **July 2026 roadmap**, @ Tagging was listed under “Coming in the next two months.” The August 2026 product-update snapshot does not provide a later status for this item, so those materials do not establish its current availability.

See the [Product Roadmap - July 2026](https://github.com/reflexivity-kb/platform/blob/main/en/04-releases/2026-07-product-roadmap.md) for the original point-in-time plan.

For current availability or entitlement, contact **jim@reflexivity.com**.
