Theme Pulses are designed to help a user follow how an investment narrative develops over time. The July roadmap called the capability **Pulses** and described a running timeline that brings together sentiment, headlines, and key quotations. The August update referred to it as **Theme Pulses** and also mentioned related macro themes.

In the **July 2026 roadmap**, Pulses was an EOY roadmap item. In the **August 2026 product update**, Theme Pulses was listed as “Readying for release.” Those labels are historical status markers rather than a current availability promise.

See [Product Roadmap - July 2026](https://github.com/reflexivity-kb/platform/blob/main/en/04-releases/2026-07-product-roadmap.md) and [Product Update Snapshot - August 2026](https://github.com/reflexivity-kb/platform/blob/main/en/04-releases/2026-08-product-update-snapshot.md).
