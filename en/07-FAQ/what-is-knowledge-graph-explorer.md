Knowledge Graph Explorer is an interactive map for exploring how companies, themes, and events connect. A user can start from a name on a watchlist and move outward through related entities and topics.

Its dated status changed quickly: the **July 2026 roadmap** placed Knowledge Graph Explorer on the EOY roadmap, while the **August 2026 product update** listed it under “Recently shipped.” This is why dated roadmap and release pages are kept separately.

See [Product Roadmap - July 2026](https://github.com/reflexivity-kb/platform/blob/main/en/04-releases/2026-07-product-roadmap.md) and [Product Update Snapshot - August 2026](https://github.com/reflexivity-kb/platform/blob/main/en/04-releases/2026-08-product-update-snapshot.md).
