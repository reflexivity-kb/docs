A live walkthrough is usually most useful when it starts from a company, theme, or question the audience already follows.

A typical live walkthrough covers four stages:

## 1. Knowledge Graph visualizer

Pick a company the audience follows and explore the relationships around it.

This makes it possible to move from a known name to connected companies, themes, products, and other exposures.

## 2. Alfred analysis

Run one or two research examples and show how an investor can move from a question into a deeper analytical workflow.

Depending on the current environment and entitlement, the walkthrough can also include:

- inline citations;
- Prompt Refiner;
- Ask / Analyst modes;
- Scheduled Queries;
- Alfred's Daily Brief.

Current availability can vary by product version and entitlement.

## 3. Insights

Review one or two examples of how Reflexivity surfaces market developments and related implications.

[Read: The Anatomy of a Reflexivity Insight](https://github.com/reflexivity-kb/platform/blob/main/en/06-articles/05-alfred-and-insights/the-anatomy-of-a-reflexivity-insight.md)

## 4. Scenario analysis

Use a scenario example to show how the research workflow can move from a market question into historical comparison, relationship analysis, or forward-looking investigation.

[Public example: Compare oil and gold through inflation, rates, and forward scenarios](https://github.com/reflexivity-kb/docs/blob/main/en/05-use-cases/03-asset-class/05-multi-asset/oil-gold-inflation-rates-scenarios.md)
