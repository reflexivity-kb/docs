> [!IMPORTANT]
> This is a **July 2026 point-in-time roadmap view**. It is not a delivery commitment or a statement of current availability.

The July 2026 roadmap covers what had recently shipped, what was planned next, and the broader product direction.

## Recently shipped: July 2026

- **Scheduled Queries**
- **Alfred's Personalized Daily Brief**
- **Microsoft 365, Teams + Copilot Integration**
- **Ask & Analyst Mode**
- **Inline Citations**

## Coming in the next two months: July 2026 plan

- [**@ Tagging in Alfred**](what-is-tagging-in-alfred.md): set company, theme, or watchlist context directly in a conversation.
- [**Export to PowerPoint & Word**](what-is-export-to-powerpoint-and-word.md): turn completed analysis into materials that can be adapted for client-ready documents.

## On the roadmap for EOY: July 2026 plan

- [**Theme Pulses / Pulses**](what-are-theme-pulses.md): follow how a narrative develops over time.
- [**Knowledge Graph Explorer**](what-is-knowledge-graph-explorer.md): explore connections among companies, themes, and events.
- [**Earnings Narrative Snapshots**](what-are-earnings-narrative-snapshots.md): read earnings as a developing narrative rather than only as figures.
- [**Prompt Library**](what-is-the-prompt-library.md): save and reuse recurring analyses as templates.

For timing and access questions, see [When will roadmap features become available?](when-will-roadmap-features-become-available.md) and [Can I request early access to upcoming features?](can-i-request-early-access-to-upcoming-features.md).
